SECTION 1 — LEGAL & SECURITY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

[NOT DONE] No privacy policy (Datenschutzerklärung)
  File: none exists. Impressum exists at src/pages/Impressum.jsx but that is
  a different legal document under German law.
  Why it matters: the contact form collects name/email/message and the site
  runs Vercel Analytics — both are personal-data processing under GDPR. A
  German TMG/DSGVO-compliant site needs a dedicated privacy policy, not just
  an Impressum. I did not draft this because it requires actual decisions
  about what data you process and how (analytics retention, whether you use
  cookies, etc.) — that's not something to invent on your behalf.
  Action needed from you: decide what to disclose, then I can build the page.

[DONE] Contact endpoint spam protection
  Files: api/contact.js, src/pages/Contact.jsx,
         src/sections/homepage-content/useContactForm.js,
         src/sections/homepage-content/HomepageContent.jsx
  Added a hidden honeypot field ("website") to both contact forms. If a bot
  fills it, the API returns a fake success (200) without sending email or
  touching credentials. Verified this short-circuit works by calling the
  handler directly with a filled honeypot field.

[DONE] Unescaped user input in HTML email
  File: api/contact.js
  Added an escapeHtml() function; name/email/message are now escaped before
  being interpolated into the HTML email body. Verified the code path runs
  cleanly through to a real (fake-credential) Gmail SMTP call without
  throwing.

[DONE] No length limits on submitted fields
  File: api/contact.js
  Added MAX_NAME_LENGTH (200) and MAX_MESSAGE_LENGTH (5000) checks.
